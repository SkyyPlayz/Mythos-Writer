title: Lantern Quay
pov: Mara Vell

Mara Vell pulled her hood down against the drizzle. Under the lamps, Mara's dark hair shone with rain.
Tomas waited by the crates. He had blue eyes that never settled.
Across the quay, Tomas, blue eyes narrowed, counted the barrels twice.
