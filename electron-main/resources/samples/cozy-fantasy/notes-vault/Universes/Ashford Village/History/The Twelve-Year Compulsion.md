# The Twelve-Year Compulsion

Twelve years ago, Conal Fenwick's mother lay dying of the forgetting tremor and asked Sister Marigold to spare her son the worst of his grief. Marigold used the family's hearth-anchor watch to remove one hour a week — the Thursday at three when his mother died — meaning it to last a year or two. It never stopped. Rowan Ashby got within a week of identifying the source before she passed; Wren finished the investigation, working with Thessaly Briarbend's accounts and Sable Nightshade's watch to trace it back to Marigold, and began the slow work of giving Fenwick his grief back.

**Role in story:** The mystery's backbone; resolves the central question of the book and its theme that good intentions without consent still cause harm.
