# Ashford Village

A small Cotswold market town where magic operates through the ordinary acts of domestic life. Ashford is less a collection of houses than a collection of relationships — Wren inherits a place in most of them the moment she takes over her grandmother's shop.

**Notable places:** the tea shop at the village edge, the mill on the millrace, the market green.

**Role in story:** The setting and, per the story notes, effectively a character in its own right.
