# The Hearthstone Tea Shop

A cottage tea shop at the edge of Ashford Village, run for forty-one years by Rowan Ashby and now inherited by her granddaughter Wren. The shop "runs itself, more or less" — a chalk-board menu updates on its own, an apron waits on its hook, and a hearth burns with a fire nobody lit. The magic here follows the warmth: brewing tea, tending the fire, and simply being present are the shop's real business.

**Notable features:** the self-updating chalk-board, the hearth where memories of Rowan surface, the hob where Pip steams.

**Role in story:** Home base and consulting room; where the village brings its problems.

**Relationships:**
- Run by [[Wren Ashby]], previously by [[Rowan Ashby]]
- Home to the familiar [[Pip]]
- Resonance sustained by The Hearth Compact
- Sits at the edge of Ashford Village
