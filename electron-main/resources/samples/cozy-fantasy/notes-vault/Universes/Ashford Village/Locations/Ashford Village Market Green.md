# Ashford Village Market Green

The green where Ashford holds its Saturday market — stalls, a herb seller, and a bookkeeper's table among them. It is where Wren first sets up Hearthstone Tea & Consultations "by appointment or by need," and where she learns that a village is a web of relationships she has already inherited a place in.

**Role in story:** Where Wren meets Thessaly Briarbend's account of the mill anomaly and first sees Sable Nightshade.

**Relationships:**
- [[Wren Ashby]] sets up her consultation table here on market day
- [[Thessaly Briarbend]] shares the mill's financial anomaly here
- [[Sable Nightshade]] is seen browsing the herb seller's stall
- Part of Ashford Village
