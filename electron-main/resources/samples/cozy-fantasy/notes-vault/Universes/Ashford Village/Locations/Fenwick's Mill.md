# Fenwick's Mill

The Fenwick family mill has ground grain on the same stone since 1847, four generations running. Conal "Old" Fenwick keeps it now, cheerful and precise about weather and water levels. Near the mill's north wheel, Sable Nightshade found a cracked pocket watch six weeks before bringing it to Wren — and it is there, every Thursday at three, that Sister Marigold has quietly wound that same watch for twelve years.

**Role in story:** Site of the mystery's mechanism; where the compulsion is discovered, wound, and ultimately broken.
