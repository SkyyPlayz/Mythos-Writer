# The Cracked Pocket Watch

An old-fashioned pocket watch, its face cracked through a line that cuts exactly across the three o'clock mark. It was Old Fenwick's mother's — a hearth-anchor, very old — and Sister Marigold used it twelve years ago to remove one hour of his grief a week, the Thursday at three when his mother died. Sable Nightshade found it near the mill's north wheel and brought it to Wren, cold with warmth deliberately removed, sour and off-key to the touch.

**Role in story:** The compulsion's mechanism; the physical proof of the mystery, and the thing Wren must learn to break gently.
