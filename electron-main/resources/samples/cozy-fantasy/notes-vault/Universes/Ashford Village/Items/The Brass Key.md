# The Brass Key

The old-fashioned brass key to the shop, notched at the bow in a shape that fits Wren's thumb "as if the metal had been waiting for exactly that thumb." It was warm when the solicitor handed it over — warmer than three days in an envelope should allow — the first sign Wren had that the inheritance was more than a cottage and a business.

**Role in story:** Opening image and inciting object; Wren's first physical contact with the inheritance.

**Relationships:**
- Passed to [[Wren Ashby]] from [[Rowan Ashby]]'s estate
- Opens [[The Hearthstone Tea Shop]]
